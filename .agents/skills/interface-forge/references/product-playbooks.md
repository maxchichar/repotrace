# Web product playbooks — choose by user task

These are investigative priorities, not preset visual templates. Use the relevant section only.

## SaaS / operations / enterprise dashboards

Optimize throughput, learnability, status confidence, auditability, permission awareness, search, bulk actions, data filtering, saved views and exception resolution. Determine decision frequency and density requirements. Show period, origin and units for metrics. Maintain deep links and table semantics. Test empty and partially failed data, pagination/sorting, multi-tenant context and slow networks.

### Multi-branch Nigerian payment reconciliation example

Staff submit receipts/statements; automated evidence extraction may be uncertain; managers approve closure. Differentiate claimed receipt from trusted bank confirmation. Show currency `NGN`/₦, transaction time/timezone, branch, payment rail, discrepancy status, evidence provenance, OCR confidence as *uncertainty* (not proof), and audit history. Staff should not acquire manager close authority through an action-menu redesign. Validate low-connectivity uploads and document edge cases. Never mark an amount settled solely from a screenshot.

## E-commerce

Make catalog search/filter and product details reliable; disclose inventory accuracy, shipping, returns, taxes, variations and full checkout totals. Preserve cart through session problems when safe. Avoid fake urgency, fake reviews or deceptive preselected options. Test mobile keyboard, address formats, failed payments, guest account flow and accessibility of variant selection.

## Landing pages / product marketing

Above the fold: actual proposition, intended user, credible differentiation and relevant action. Order problem → proposed mechanism → concrete product proof → objections → CTA, adapting to complexity of purchase. Avoid fake social proof. Include live product examples only when real. Optimize for content clarity, SEO, actual conversion instrumenting and load speed. Not every brand needs a giant hero or feature-card row.

## Portfolios / editorial / creator brands

Show point of view, selected work, truthful case-study outcomes, writing and a frictionless contact path. Express character through type, composition, original imagery and editorial rhythm rather than trendy components. Ensure narrative hierarchy, scannable projects, readable paragraphs, image loading and accessible page navigation.

## Finance / health / government and trust-critical workflows

Safety, understanding and traceability outweigh novelty. Disclose what is authoritative, current, estimated or missing. Prevent harmful ambiguity, unauthorized operations and irreversible actions; make forms recoverable. Consider language, device access, low bandwidth, screen reader use and evidence retention. Meet applicable regulations through product/legal review rather than assuming UI implementation creates compliance.

## Admin panels / back office

Control density intentionally. Make batch operations previewable, destructive operations confirmable, permission scoping visible, data sortable/filterable, operational errors actionable. Validate critical procedures end-to-end with real roles.
